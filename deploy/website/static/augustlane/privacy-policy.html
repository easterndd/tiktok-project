2 Information We Process

Depending on the Mini and features used, we may process a locally generated anonymous identifier; a TikTok-provided identifier or authorization data if platform sign-in is used; viewing history, playback progress, favorites, preferences and episode unlock records; advertisement requests, display, completion and error signals; IP address, approximate country, device and network information, logs, diagnostics, launch and attribution parameters, and information you send when contacting us. We do not require a separate Service password or payment-card details to watch short dramas. TikTok and advertising providers may process information under their own notices.

3 Purposes

We process this information to deliver playback, remember progress and unlock status, operate advertising-supported access, verify rewards, secure the Service, prevent abuse, measure entry sources, diagnose errors, respond to requests and comply with law. Where required, processing relies on contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations or consent as applicable.

4 Sharing and Transfers

We may share information with TikTok, video delivery, hosting, advertising, security and support providers, authorized personnel, or authorities where required by law. We do not sell personal information for money. Our application hosting may process data in the United States; providers may process data in other jurisdictions. We use safeguards where required by law.

5 Retention and Security

We remove raw IP addresses from app-session records after 30 days; country-level session data may remain for reporting. Other information is retained only as reasonably necessary for service operation, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. We apply reasonable safeguards, but no online service is completely secure.

7 Children
